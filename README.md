By day (or more honestly, by night — I do my best work after dark), I work on pharmacogenomics pipelines, HPC infrastructure, and large-scale genomic data. I also write theology, build platforms, and think about the fallen nature of man with the same rigor I bring to variant calling.

## What I Work On

### 🧬 Genomics & Bioinformatics
Large-scale pharmacogenomics research at ACE-IDI under the SHEDS Programme. My current focus is a multi-technology comparison study — long-read sequencing, WGS, WES, and genotyping arrays — across CYP450 genes using 1,019 samples from the 1000 Genomes ONT dataset. I work daily with VCF/CRAM files, GATK, VEP, PyPGx, PharmCAT, SLURM job arrays, and bcftools on HPC clusters.

Previously: a complete WES pharmacogenomics pipeline across AFR, EAS, EUR, and SAS populations; VEP annotation with ClinVar, dbNSFP 5.3.1a, and LoFtool; PyPGx patching for Python 3.12 compatibility.

**Tools I use:** Linux/HPC · SLURM · R · Python · GATK · VEP · PyPGx · PharmCAT · minimap2 · bcftools · Git

### ✦ Sela-Hi — *Word. Psych. Meditate.*
A Christian word-meditation platform I'm building from scratch. It combines a hand-curated dataset of 771 theological meditation words, AI-powered semantic Bible search over 31,102 KJV verses, and a depth-psychology framework (Jungian archetypes) as a lens for honest self-examination before Scripture. Built with Next.js, Supabase, and Vercel.

### ✍️ Writing & Theology

## Background

I am a graduate Nurse from Kabale University before pivoting into bioinformatics. My undergraduate research was on yellow fever vaccine uptake. I've since worked on scoping reviews (gene fusions in ALL; AlphaFold in drug discovery), mixed-methods study protocol development, and the BEWASETF/SHEDS Programme studying women's advancement in data-intensive sciences.

I didn't come to computing through the traditional route. I came through patient care, then data, then code — and I think that order matters. It means I never forget what the numbers are actually about.
